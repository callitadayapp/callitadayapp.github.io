Call It a Day

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

The short version: Call It a Day doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your activities, entries, rewards, history and settings are stored by the app on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We can't see your data. Like any app's data, it's included in your iPhone's own backups to iCloud or a computer, under your Apple Account.

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file, and you choose where to save it. The app doesn't upload it anywhere.

Purchases

Plus and tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether you have Plus.

App lock

Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Reminders

The reminder to close your day is scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Sharing

Share cards are images made on your iPhone. They go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet.

Feedback

"Send feedback" opens a draft in your mail app. You see the whole message and send it yourself. Besides your words, the draft includes the app version, device model, iOS version and language, to help us reproduce bugs. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and to fix things, and we don't share them. Ask us and we'll delete your emails.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all its data from your iPhone. Backup files you saved stay where you put them.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, for example by syncing through iCloud, this page will change first and the date above will be updated.
