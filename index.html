Call It a Day

Do the work, earn your evening

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone

The things you keep putting off pay for the things you love.

Work, study and the gym earn you free time. Games, the feed and one more episode spend it. Do what matters, then enjoy the rest, knowing you've earned it. Nothing is banned, and nobody nags you.

How it works

  • Set your prices. Every activity has a rate, and every rate is yours. A rule that works: whatever you keep putting off should pay well.
  • Log as you go. Type the minutes, pick a tier, or run a timer that stays on your Lock Screen.
  • Spend it. A game, a show, a lazy evening: it costs what it costs, and then it's paid for, with nothing left to argue about.
  • Call it a day. Close the day, see what it earned, and collect what it unlocked.

Yours, on your phone

No account, no ads, no server. No app blocking and no Screen Time permissions: it trusts you. Everything stays on your iPhone, and you can back it up to a file whenever you like.

Where it came from

I kept this system in a spreadsheet for ten years. Call It a Day is what it grew into: the same rules, without the spreadsheet.
