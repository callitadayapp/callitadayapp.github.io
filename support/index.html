Call It a Day

Support

Questions, bugs, ideas: write to callitadayapp@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings → Send feedback in the app: it adds the app version and device details for you.

How things work

Settings → FAQ explains points, streaks, rewards and the rest.

Moving to a new iPhone

Settings → Export backup on the old phone, then Settings → Restore from backup on the new one.

Purchases

To get Plus back on a new device, open the Plus card in Settings and tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
